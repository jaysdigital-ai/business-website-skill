# Quality bar

The standard every demo must meet, taken from demos that prospects and the plugin's author approved (e.g. "Southerly Air Co.", a 9-page HVAC demo). This is a **quality** benchmark, not a design to copy: every demo still gets its own look (see section 1b of the skill).

## What "good" looked like in an approved demo

- **9 pages**, each with its own animated hero plus **5 to 10 further sections**. The home page had 11 sections, inner pages 6 to 10. No page was a stub.
- **Concrete content everywhere:** services with what's included and "from" prices ($189 service, $1,890 install), a price table, a 3-tier plan comparison, team bios with roles, a dated company timeline, project case studies with numbers (36 zones, 31% lower energy), 4 to 5 FAQs per page, opening hours, service-area suburbs, a working 4-step booking form, a savings calculator, filterable project gallery.
- **Home hero that tells the service story:** a photo slideshow (slow zoom, crossfade, faces kept in frame), a rotating headline, and a row of live cards *below* the photo: a thermostat dial counting to the set temperature, a technician ETA countdown with progress bar, a service checklist ticking itself off, plus one "next available" pill in a corner.
- **A different hero on every page:** cursor-following tilted photo stack (About), morphing photo blob with orbiting service icons (Services), live building zone-temperature board (Commercial), filling system-health gauge (Plans), draggable before/after slider (Projects), shuffling article card deck (Advice), map with pulsing rings, bouncing pin and moving vans (Contact), a booking card that fills itself in (Book).
- **Motion with restraint:** word-by-word headline reveals, clip-reveal photos with gentle parallax, staggered cards, drawn step lines, counting stats, scaling CTA panels, a ticker band, desktop card tilt and magnetic buttons, a reading-progress bar, an industry-themed ambient 3D layer. All of it off for reduced motion, with a fallback that reveals everything.
- **Photos:** real, full-resolution, sharp at 2x, each use cropped deliberately; team portraits cropped from group shots; mismatched subjects replaced with icon cards.
- **Typography and spacing:** a distinctive display face matched to the logo plus a clean body face, fluid sizes with clamp(), generous section padding (80 to 150px desktop), consistent card radius and shadows, a giant wordmark footer.
- **Mobile:** deliberate layouts (stacked heroes, two-up cards, hidden secondary cards), no sideways scroll, menu overlay that works.
- **Trust details:** sample-labelled testimonials, licences and accreditations strip, guarantees, emergency line, fictional-details note in the footer.

## Scoring rubric (used by the website-tester)

Score each criterion 1 to 5. **Every criterion must score 4 or 5 to pass.**

| Criterion | 5 means |
|---|---|
| Premium feel | Looks like a studio-built site; nothing reads as a template or AI default |
| Uniqueness | Clearly different from every demo in the design log on at least 6 of 10 axes |
| Completeness | Every page has its hero plus 5+ real sections; no placeholders, stubs or lorem ipsum |
| Content depth | Prices, inclusions, numbers, FAQs, hours, team, booking path, all specific to the industry |
| Photography | Real, sharp, well-cropped photos; faces never covered; no mismatched images |
| Heroes and motion | A distinct on-theme hero per page; motion smooth, purposeful and never blocking reading |
| Typography and layout | Consistent scale, rhythm and alignment across pages; strong hierarchy |
| Mobile | Every page deliberate at 390px; no overflow; tap targets at least 44px |
| Accessibility | AA contrast, alt text, labelled forms, focus styles, reduced motion respected |
| SEO | Unique titles and descriptions, one H1, valid LocalBusiness and FAQ schema, sitemap |

If a criterion scores 3 or lower, name the gap and the owner, and send it back for fixing.
